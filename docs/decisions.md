| 44 | setup-mstack | Skill hash: SHA-256 over every file of the folder, as sorted relative POSIX paths plus contents, ignoring `.DS_Store` and `__pycache__`. | 45 | setup-mstack | A **corrupt** manifest makes every present mstack-named skill `modified-locally`.
